# Weekly service review

## Prepare the evidence

Run the pipeline and tests, inspect the source hash, reconcile 141,712 snapshots to 24,918 cases, then open the weekly metrics and final-group table. Separate observations from staffing assumptions.

## Review with service leadership

1. What is changing in intake, closure volume and backlog? Which complete periods are comparable?
2. Which groups have frequent handoffs or reopenings? Inspect samples before labeling root causes.
3. Does the mean4 forecast outperform the last-week benchmark? If not, retain the simpler benchmark for discussion and investigate coverage changes.
4. What measured handling-time and skill data are needed before translating a scenario into a resource plan?
5. Which recurring question could a training guide or knowledge article address? Record an owner, due date and observable success measure before rollout.

## Enablement walkthrough

Explain event-versus-case grain, reproduce one week by SQL, configure a capacity parameter and interpret the resulting scenario. Have the learner distinguish elapsed resolution time from active handling time.

## Product feedback template

Capture problem, affected workflow, evidence/query, customer impact, proposed change, owner, validation gate and rollback condition. Use reproducible incident evidence before escalating a feature request.
